1. Introduction

Welcome to Policy, Wellness and Conflict Resolution ("App"). We aim to align our processing practices with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA), including the core principles of accountability, processing limitation, purpose specification, further processing limitation, information quality, openness, security safeguards, and data subject participation.

Where we process personal information on behalf of a business, that business is responsible for ensuring it has a lawful basis to collect and share the information with us.

4. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: Name, email address, role, company affiliation, department, and login credentials
  • Profile Data: Job title, team assignments, and preferences
  • Training Data: Quiz responses, completion status, scores, reading progress, and acknowledgments
  • Communication Data: Messages in chat, inbox messages, support requests, and feedback
  • Business Content: Policies, procedures, safety documents, training documents, and compliance records

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version. We do not collect device or other identifiers through the app.
  • Usage Data: Features accessed, time spent, interaction patterns, and login timestamps
  • Technical Data: IP address, crash logs, and diagnostic information

5. How We Use Information

  • Provide account access, authentication, and role-based permissions
  • Deliver policy content, quizzes, dashboards, and compliance tracking
  • Send reminders, alerts, and in-app notifications about overdue quizzes or account activity
  • Support managers and supervisors with weekly performance reporting and follow-up information
  • Improve app features, reliability, security, and user experience
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and policy violations
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, tax, and audit obligations

6. Lawful Processing and Business Data

We process personal information only for legitimate and specified purposes. For business users, this may include employee training, compliance monitoring, policy distribution, and reporting to managers or supervisors. We limit access to the minimum information needed for these purposes.

Where required, we rely on consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation, legitimate interests, or a business's lawful instruction to process data.

7. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only where necessary and appropriate with:

  • Your Company/Manager: Training status, quiz participation, compliance records, and related performance statistics
  • Service Providers: Cloud hosting, database, analytics, notifications, and AI processing providers under confidentiality and security obligations
  • Legal Authorities: Where required by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, or lawful request
  • Professional Support Contacts: When a user is referred to a counselor, lawyer, or other support contact made available by the business

8. Security Safeguards

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information and business information, including:

  • Encrypted transmission using HTTPS/TLS
  • Access controls and role-based permissions
  • Hashed passwords and authentication protections
  • Audit trails and activity tracking
  • Secure hosting and restricted administrative access
  • Regular review of access and security settings

While we work to protect data, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Businesses remain responsible for securing their own credentials, devices, and internal access controls.

9. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain records required for business compliance.

When an account is closed or data is deleted, we may retain limited information for legal, audit, or security purposes where required or permitted by law.
